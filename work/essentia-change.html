Change the Equation

A guerrilla-style NYC takeover of fitness spaces, built for social.

Client
Essentia Water
Type
Social Campaign
Role
Producer
Year
2024
essentia-change-hero.jpg

Essentia's Change the Equation campaign featured a NYC takeover at fitness spaces like Action Black Nomad, plus collaborations with street and fitness influencers to weave the brand into the city's active lifestyle scene.

Fabrication included a custom jacket that held about 20 Essentia water bottles, a branded basketball, a skateboard, an enormous shopping bag, bucket drums, delivery boxes, a newspaper, boxing gloves and a punching bag.

Shot guerrilla-style with a skeleton crew, built specifically for social.
