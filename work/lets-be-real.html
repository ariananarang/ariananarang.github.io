Let's Be Real

Films about being there for a friend, told entirely through body language.

Client
Ad Council Seize the Awkward
Type
Film
Role
Producer
Year
2025
lets-be-real-hero.jpg
When your friends are struggling with their mental health, they need a real friend to be there for them. To be real with them.

The campaign is a set of stripped-back films that depict authentic support through visual storytelling rather than dialogue.

The narrative emerges through body language — hesitations, nervous laughter, eye contact, and the subtle physical shifts that communicate presence and care.
