Pawn Shop

A Cinco de Mayo popup pawn shop trading offensive merch for hard seltzer.

Type

Role
Producer

topochico-hero.jpg
Margaritas from a hollowed out watermelon. "Cinco de Drinko." Margaritaville. The margarita has become victim to basic drinking culture.

The campaign aimed to position the brand's margarita hard seltzer as the innovative alternative.

A popup pawn shop launched on Cinco de Mayo at 7th St & 1st Ave in NYC, where customers could exchange offensive holiday-themed merchandise for the new product.
